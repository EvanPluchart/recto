A quote, from inbox to signature.

A joiner emails you a quote as a PDF. Here is what happens in Recto, step by step, without leaving your computer.

Saved: quote-2026-118.pdf

  1. It opens for reading.

    Like a viewer: read, zoom, search for a word, print. Nothing moves by mistake, and a double-click opens a PDF straight from File Explorer.

  2. Edit brings out the tools.

    A toolbar and the page thumbnails appear. Every tool has its key: T for text, H to highlight, S to sign.

  3. Highlight what matters.

    The lead time, in yellow. Highlights, underlines, shapes, arrows and notes stay real PDF annotations that other software can read.

  4. Sign with your own name.

    Type it here: it is written on the quote, in the style and ink you choose. Nothing is sent, not even from this page.

  5. Redact before you forward it.

    Your address does not need to travel with the quote. Under the black box, the text is removed from the saved file: it can no longer be copied or found by searching.

  6. Save, and you are done.

    The file is rewritten in one go, never half-written. It stayed on your disk from start to finish: no account, no quota, no connection.

Questions before you install

Does Recto send my files anywhere?

No. Recto has no account and no server: it reads your files from your disk and saves them in the same place, with an atomic write that never leaves a PDF half-written. It works offline.

Is it really free?

Yes, under the MIT licence, with no ads, no paid tier and no limit on pages or tasks.

Why does Windows show a warning when I install it?

The installer is not yet signed with a code-signing certificate, so SmartScreen flags it as uncommon. Click "More info", then "Run anyway". It installs for your account only, without administrator rights, and uninstalls cleanly.

What about macOS or Linux?

Yes, as a preview: a macOS app for Apple Silicon Macs (M1 and later) and an AppImage for x86_64 Linux, on the same download page. They build and pass the automated tests, but nobody has tried them by hand yet. The macOS app is not signed: the first time, right-click Recto, then choose Open.

Is the redaction reliable?

Text, images and drawings under a black box are removed from the file, not just hidden. Two limits: a path that sticks out of the box stays under it, and the document's accessibility structure is removed. Always check the exported file.

Can I edit the text already in a PDF?

Yes, in beta. Recto rewrites the line with the original font when it has the letters needed, otherwise with a close font from your system. Text does not reflow: a longer line can overlap what follows. Text recognition (OCR) only reads unaccented letters for now.
